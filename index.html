🎮 HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS

🕹️ Vehicle Controls

  • W / ▲ : Accelerate / Gas
  • S / ▼ : Brake / Reverse
  • A / ◄ : Steer Left
  • D / ► : Steer Right
  • SPACE : Handbrake (Initiates High-Angle Power Drifts!)
  • SHIFT : Nitro Boost (NOS)
  • H : Car Horn
  • L : Toggle Headlights

⚡ Hotkeys & Menus

  • C : Toggle 3D Camera (Chase, Close, Hood, Heli)
  • G : Garage & Car Upgrades
  • R : Street Race Circuits
  • M : Full City Map
  • ESC : Close Open Windows

🔥 Features & Pro Tips

• Drifting: Tap Handbrake while turning at speed to slide. Build huge drift combos to bank bonus cash!

• Stunt Ramps: Hit yellow chevron ramps at high speed to perform airborne leaps across city streets!

• Wanted Stars: Crashing into civilians or speeding past radars spawns Police Interceptors. Outrun them or break sight to evade!

• Collectibles: Grab green cash briefcases ($) and cyan NOS canisters hidden across alleys, docks, and islands!
